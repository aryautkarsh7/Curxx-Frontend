About Dr. Priya Sharma

Dr. Priya Sharma is a distinguished Senior Dermatologist and Dermatosurgeon with over 14 years of clinical excellence across leading institutes including AIIMS New Delhi and Manipal Hospitals. She specializes in evidence-based management of recalcitrant adult acne, pediatric dermatoses, advanced fractional laser revisions, and hair loss protocols. What happens during the initial consultation with Dr. Priya Sharma? expand_more

Can I reschedule or cancel my consultation if an emergency occurs? expand_more

Yes. Full refunds and instantaneous 1-click slot rescheduling are permitted up to 2 hours prior to the booked appointment window. Cancellations made inside 2 hours are subject to a nominal clinic desk fee of ₹100.

Are prescriptions automatically synchronized with my ABHA Health Locker? expand_more

Yes. Curxx is fully certified with the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM). Upon completion of your clinic or video visit, your digitally signed medical receipt and diagnosis are pushed immediately to your 14-digit ABHA ID address.

Every booking includes a 7-day complimentary chat window with Dr. Priya Sharma's clinical care team. You can clarify dosage schedules, upload progress photographs, or ask questions regarding topical medication reactions at no extra cost.
